> **2026-07-27 — P1-W5 census join.** FR-1.5 → **Done**. The join coverage is **99.9926%** of served rows (99.9970% of record-type-1 structures), with all 55 misses quarantined by reason. Two findings changed what gets documented rather than what gets built. First, the serving table holds 117,781 rows that are not structures — the routes NBI publishes *under* a bridge — and every one of them carries no condition rating, so any count taken from `core.bridge` without a record-type filter over-states "bridges" by 19% and fills the Unknown bucket with things that were never rated. Coverage is therefore published against both denominators, and FR-1.4's rankings and report card count record type 1. Second, the Connecticut planning-region trap is worse and simpler than the staging README predicted: NBI never adopted the 2022 regions at all, so it is not the older half of the series that fails to join but **every Connecticut row in every vintage** — 5,644 served rows, 4,362 of them structures, under 8 retired codes. The README claim was corrected here; the identical claim in `tools/trends/trends.sql` remains queued as its own docs-only change.
>
> **2026-07-26 — P1-W3 condition trends.** FR-1.2 → **Done**.
